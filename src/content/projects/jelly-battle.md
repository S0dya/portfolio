An old **Flash game**, remade for PC to show whether its multiplayer mechanic was
worth taking further.

This was a short gig: a prototype built in a few weeks, not a full game. I wrote
the architecture and the gameplay code on top of **Photon Fusion** for the
realtime networking.

The prototype was delivered, but the mechanic did not justify being built out
further - which is why the project is closed.
