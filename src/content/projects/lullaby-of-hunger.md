**Genre:** Survival · **Made with:** Unity · **Platforms:** Windows

## Assets

All the 3D assets and most of the animations were made completely on my own in
Blender - modeling, rigging and animation.

The game uses animation rigging, which is what allows the interactive shooting and
reloading in combat.

Audio is FMOD, wired up alongside the animation so events and the shots they trigger
stay in step.
